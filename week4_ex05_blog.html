My Tech Blog

This page is about my two favourite technologies: Artificial Intelligence (AI) and cloud computing.

Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence brings major benifits across industries such as automating processes by handling routine, time-consuming duties much faster than humans. It's 24 hour availability and support systems operate without without fatigue, sleep, or breaks to deliver instant responses. AI minimises mistakes, helping improve accuracy in complex tasks. It processes vast volumes of data in seconds, uncovering hidden patterns to guide better strategic choices.

Cloud Computing

Cloud computing offeres businesses and individuals significant advantages such as flexibility, cost savings and scalability. It reduces capital expenses by eliminating the need to buy and maintain expensive physical hardware and data centers, shifting costs to a pay-as-you-go model. Cloud computing easily scales resources up or down instantly to handle traffic or workload changes without needing to upgrade infrastructures. It saves time by automatically delivering software updates, security patches, andd maintenance directly from the provider.
